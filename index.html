// straight answers

The no-slang FAQ.

Everything above, in plain English.

What does Atwias do?

Atwias Info Solutions is a Chennai-based agency that runs four disciplines as one team: branding and content, performance marketing (Meta, Google and WhatsApp ads, SEO), sales enablement and CRM, and web, app and AI development. We measure success in enquiries, site visits, walk-ins, admissions and sales.

What is an agentic growth studio?

It means we use AI agents for repetitive work: drafting creative variations, watching campaign spend and lead quality, replying first to new enquiries, keeping follow-ups on track and writing weekly reports. People on our team approve your brand voice, budgets and anything sent to your customers.

Which industries does Atwias work with?

Real estate, education, retail and D2C, FMCG, mattresses and home, healthcare, entertainment and media, and leisure. Clients include Disney+ Hotstar, Aachi Group, GP Homes, VGP Universal Kingdom, SRM, Vels University, Vasanth & Co, Lyca Productions, Revolt and StartupTN.

What is Neuraura?

Neuraura is a CRM built by Atwias for sales teams that work on enquiries. Each company gets its own workspace for leads, calls, follow-ups, pipeline and site visits. It is a separate product at neuraura.ai and can be used with or without Atwias.

Can Atwias help my brand show up in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI answers?

Yes. Our Prompt Presence service, also called generative engine optimisation (GEO), checks how AI assistants describe your brand today and fixes the content and structured data they rely on, then keeps checking over time.

How do engagements and pricing work?

Every engagement starts with a discovery call. We agree scope, timeline and pricing after that, and media spend and platform subscriptions are billed separately. We work with monthly budgets from under ₹1 lakh to more than ₹10 lakh.
